Be Found at the Moment Someone Is Looking.

Practical digital marketing for small businesses in Kerala — search, maps, campaigns and simple tracking, built around a budget that makes sense for a business your size.

Digital presence for local businesses

Start with the basics, done properly.

If your business is new to the internet, or has been online without anyone paying attention, this is the sensible place to begin. It is unglamorous, it is quick, and it is what makes every other marketing activity work harder.

  • Business profile setup and cleanup. Correct name, address, category, hours, photos and services listed properly.
  • Platform guidance. A short, honest recommendation of which two or three channels are worth your time — and which to ignore.
  • Basic content strategy. A simple plan for what to post, how often, and what to photograph.
  • Website and social coordination. The same details, the same tone and the same contact points everywhere.
  • Adoption support. Practical help setting up email, WhatsApp Business and analytics so you can see what is happening.

Ask us to look at your current setup

"Near me" searches are how a lot of people choose a shop now. Being correctly listed and correctly categorised is most of that work.

Most local businesses are one or two people making every decision. Good marketing for them is simple, measurable and affordable.

Who this is for

For businesses that want enquiries, not followers.

We measure success by the conversations this creates — a call, a WhatsApp message, a visit to the shop. Numbers on a dashboard are useful, but they are not the point.

  • You have never advertised online. You want to start small, with one channel and a clear goal.
  • You have tried it and it did not work. The last agency took the money and left you with a report you could not read.
  • Your competitors keep appearing first. They have a listing, a website and posts, and you have a name and a number.
  • You want to know which channel deserves the budget. Right now you are guessing.
  • You need someone to explain the options. The packages on the internet all sound similar and mean different things.

How it works

Measure, learn, adjust.

Marketing that is never measured is guesswork with a budget attached. This is the loop we work in.

  1. Audit

    We review your listings, site, search presence and competitors, and write down the gaps.

  2. Prioritise

    We rank the gaps by what is likely to bring enquiries soonest, and start there.

  3. Execute

    We fix, publish and run the work — in small batches, so results arrive early and often.

  4. Review and refine

    We look at what the numbers actually say, keep the good, and change what is not working.
