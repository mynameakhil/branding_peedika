How it works

Clear steps, and a site you can keep updating.

We build simple, standard websites that you or a developer can edit without breaking anything.

  1. Scope

    We agree on the pages, the content and the one action you want visitors to take.

  2. Structure and design

    We map the pages, write the headings, and design the layouts before any of the heavy lifting starts.

  3. Build

    We build the site, connect the forms and phone links, and load real content and photographs.

  4. Launch and handover

    We test on real phones, publish it, connect it to your domain, and show you how to update it.

Before we start

What helps us move quickly.

Content is the part that slows most website projects down. Getting it together early is the single biggest help.

Content to share

  • Your services, products and prices
  • Business story, background and team details
  • Photographs of the work, premises and people
  • Opening hours, service area and contact details
  • Common questions customers ask you

Decisions to make

  • Your domain name, or whether you already have one
  • The languages the site should be in
  • What the main call to action should be
  • Whether you want to write the content or have us draft it

On hosting. We build the site as plain, portable HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so you are not locked into one platform. You can host it anywhere, and moving it later is a folder copy rather than a rebuild.
