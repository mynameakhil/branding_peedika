Who this is for

Built for businesses that have outgrown a temporary look.

Most of the businesses we meet are not starting from nothing — they have a real product and real customers. What they are missing is a look that holds together.

  • You look different everywhere. The board, the menu, the WhatsApp status and the Instagram grid each have their own colours and fonts.
  • Your first impression is not the one you planned. Customers see the logo before they read the name, and it is not saying what you sell.
  • You are adding a new line or a new outlet. Everything that exists needs to scale onto a board, a pack or a delivery menu.
  • You have outgrown a freelance logo. One file, no fonts, no rules — and no way to hand the work to someone else.
  • You are about to advertise. Ads need a consistent identity, or the same spend produces two different impressions.

Tell us where your brand feels inconsistent

A shop board is the first thing a customer sees. The identity is what makes the board, the menu and the online post look like they belong to the same business.

How it works

Four steps from confusion to a clear identity.

You will always know which step we are on, and exactly what we need from you next.

  1. Discover

    We look at your business, your competitors and your customers, and agree on what the identity needs to achieve.

  2. Direct

    We present two or three distinct directions, each with a clear rationale you can react to rather than just "like" or "dislike".

  3. Refine

    Your feedback goes into the chosen direction, and we build out the palette, type, templates and guidelines around it.

  4. Hand over

    You receive organised source files, exports and a short guide, plus a walkthrough so you can use it without us.

Before we start

What helps us move quickly.

Nothing here is compulsory. Each item simply removes a round of questions.

Useful to have

  • Your business name and the services you want to lead with
  • Two or three brands you admire, and why
  • Any existing logo, board, card or packaging you are happy with
  • The places the identity will appear — board, menu, van, Instagram, site
  • Colours you already use or want to keep

Worth deciding first

  • Who your customer is — the kind of person, not just "everyone"
  • The one thing you want a customer to remember
  • Whether the name is final
  • Your launch date, and what has to exist by then
  • Who will approve decisions if you are not available

Timing note. Branding is the slowest part of a launch because it is the part that is hardest to redo. We would rather spend the extra week getting the direction right than hand you a logo you have to live with for the next five years.
